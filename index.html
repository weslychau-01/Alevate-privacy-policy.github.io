Alevate – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 24 November 2025

Introduction

Alevate (“the App”) is designed to help alert user and fellow hikers if anyone's vital signs are abnormal, also if the user or fellow hikers are out of range. Vital signs specifically refer to heart rate and blood oxygen levels. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and how it is kept secure.

Information We Collect

1. Heart Rate Data

Used to provide real-time feedback about your physical activity and wellness during hiking sessions.

2. Blood Oxygen (SpO₂) Data

Used to notify you if blood oxygen appears lower than your typical range. Our app will not measure automatically. You will have to use the built-in Blood Oxygen app on your Apple Watch to measure this, and our app when activated tracks and monitors upon each measurement.

3. CoreBluetooth

Used solely for communication with compatible Bluetooth devices. Alevate does not use Bluetooth to track or identify users.

How We Use Your Information

Your data is used only on your device to:

  • Monitor heart rate and blood oxygen levels
  • Provide general wellness insights
  • Support real-time hiking safety features
  • Improve user experience within the App

Alevate does not provide medical advice and is not intended for diagnostic or treatment purposes.

No Data Leaves Your Device

Alevate:

  • Does not upload data to any server
  • Does not store data in the cloud
  • Does not share data with third parties
  • Does not allow developers to access your health information
  • Stores all health data securely using Apple’s HealthKit protections (if used)

HealthKit Disclosure

If HealthKit is used, the App accesses data only with explicit permission, and strictly on-device. Data is never used for advertising or analytics and never transferred off-device.

Bluetooth Usage

CoreBluetooth is used only to connect with compatible accessories. No personal data is collected or shared.

Children’s Privacy

Alevate is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from children.

Security

The App relies on Apple’s secure ecosystem and on-device encryption to protect your data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
